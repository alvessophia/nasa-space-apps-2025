Welcome to Globotany!

1) Choose the region to analyze

The function of Globotany is to analyze NASA images and assess the vegetation bloom year by year. Navigate the map by dragging, zoom with a pinch gesture, and position the area of interest. Due to image availability, the dates are fixed to 2020 and 2023. When you are within the desired frame, tap Capture (button in the footer).

2) Check if the capture is clean

After tapping Capture, verify that the region has good visibility and low cloud cover. If everything is correct, tap Analyze. The analysis may take more than a minute. Wait without closing the page (the button is located in the footer).

3) Receive the AI ​​report

Globotany's AI system generates a report comparing the images. With the result on the screen, you can export to PDF by tapping Print.

4) Export the PDF

Select a file name and save the AI-generated report. Done!
